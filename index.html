How a signal fires (the whole rulebook)
  1. Two SuperTrends — tight (10, ×1.5) and loose (11, ×2.5) — must BOTH flip to the same side. One signal per regime, maximum.
  2. On the entry bar the close must be on the signal's side of the 20-bar EMA (up to 5 bars of grace after the flip for the gates to catch up).
  3. OBV's 10-bar direction must not contradict the side — no signal against the volume flow.
  4. Plan attached at entry: stop = the loose trail, target = entry ± 2R. Outcomes replayed bar-by-bar: stop first when one bar spans both.

⚠ This is our own open-source reconstruction of the style — NOT IncomeSharks' script, and signals will not match theirs. In-sample replay stats are a sanity check, not proof of edge. Educational only; the Final Call and discipline guard stay the referee.
