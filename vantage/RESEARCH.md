## Caveats and gaps

Coverage gaps are the biggest caveat: of the four research areas requested, only area 1 (Tulip facts) and one datapoint in area 3 (Deloitte budget stat) produced verified claims. Area 2 (competitor-specific attack lines and counters for Siemens Opcenter, Rockwell Plex, Dassault Apriso, AVEVA, iBase-t, Ignition, Redzone, Parsable, L2L, Poka, Augmentir, SAP DM) yielded nothing beyond the IDC 'dissatisfied with traditional MES vendors' line, and area 4 (SDR cold-calling benchmarks — connect rates, call windows, opener data, touches-to-meeting) produced zero verified claims; the app's content in those sections remains unverified. Second, nearly all Tulip proof points are vendor-published or vendor-commissioned (press releases, case studies, the 2023 Forrester TEI) — accurate as claims about what Tulip reports, but SDRs should attribute them as such; none are independently audited. Third, specific stale-content risks identified: the 'TICO 2x production' figure is not on Tulip's current case page (webinar-only sourcing); the DMG MORI case page supports no defect percentage; the TEI figures are from May 2023; and the case-studies index does not list AstraZeneca, Johnson & Johnson, Sartorius, or Richemont (refuted 0-3 as index-verifiable — these must be re-verified against other Tulip pages/press releases or removed). Time-sensitivity: scale and funding figures date to Jan 2026, TEI to May 2023, IDC MarketScape to Nov/Dec 2024 — all were current as of July 2026 but should be rechecked if Tulip publishes 2026 updates.
